4. Crew rules – zero tolerance for objectionable content

Crews are closed groups for anglers who respect each other. Before you create or join a crew, you agree to these rules. The following are not allowed in crews:

  • insults, threats, harassment or hate speech
  • sexual, violent or otherwise objectionable content
  • cruelty to animals or photos that show fish without respect
  • advertising, spam or fraud
  • revealing other people’s swims, names or photos without their permission
  • content that breaks the law or fishery rules

You can report any catch and block any person. We review reported content within 24 hours. We remove content that breaks the rules; anyone who breaks them may be excluded from crews and from CarpD. The boss and admins of a crew can remove members.
