Privacy notice

This privacy notice explains how we handle your personal data when you use carpd.de, sign up for the waiting list or as a test angler, or use the CarpD app. It is written for users in the United Kingdom and follows the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. As we are based in Germany, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR) applies to us as well. A German version is available here.

2. This website

This website uses no cookies, no third-party analytics or advertising services and loads no content from third-party servers (for example, no Google Fonts). It only contacts our own server (Supabase, Frankfurt am Main, see 6.6) – for sign-ups, the number of free test angler places and the visitor counter below. For the help page with the AI assistant, see 4c and 4d.

Our own visitor counter. So that we know how many people visit carpd.de and where they come from (for example via an advert or a trade show), the page reports to our server when it opens (Supabase, data centre in Frankfurt am Main, Germany, see 6.6): which page, German or English page, your browser language, whether it is a phone, the source from the link (e.g. “utm_source=meta”) and the domain of the page you came from. We do not store your IP address. To avoid counting a visitor twice on the same day, we create a checksum from IP address, browser identifier and date that changes every day – so nobody can be recognised across days. Nothing is stored on your device, there are no cookies and nothing is passed to third parties. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in measuring the reach of the website and our advertising (Article 6(1)(f) UK GDPR). We delete the entries after 14 months.

If you write to us at test@carpd.de, we process your email address and the details in your message (e.g. whether you use an iPhone or Android phone) to invite you to the test and talk to you about it. Our lawful basis is taking steps at your request (Article 6(1)(b) UK GDPR). We delete the data when the test has ended, at the latest 6 months after the end of the test, unless you expressly ask to stay informed.

You can withdraw it at any time using the unsubscribe link in every email or by writing to info@carpd.de; we then delete your details immediately. If you do not confirm, we send you one reminder after about one day (no advertising, only the confirmation link). Unconfirmed addresses are deleted automatically after 30 days. The list is stored on our server at Supabase, Inc. in the EU (data centre in Frankfurt am Main, Germany, see 6.6); emails are sent via Mailjet (see above). Both act under data processing agreements.

4c. Help assistant “Ask CarpD”

On carpd.de/hilfe you can ask our assistant “Ask CarpD” (Kim) questions about CarpD. It answers only from our help texts. The answers are generated by AI and are provided without guarantee.

Your question first goes to our server at Supabase, Inc. (data centre in Frankfurt am Main, see 6.6). From there we send it to the AI service of Anthropic (Anthropic Ireland, Limited, Dublin, Ireland). Only the text of your question, the previous messages of the conversation (at most the last six) and the language are transmitted – not your IP address and no other information about you. Anthropic processes the requests on our behalf (data processing agreement), does not use them to train its AI and, according to Anthropic, deletes them after 30 days at the latest. The requests may also be processed by the parent company Anthropic, PBC in the USA; standard data protection clauses apply to this.

So that we can see what is asked often and improve the help, we store the text of the question, the language, whether the assistant could answer and which help pages it suggested – without IP address, without name and without any identifier that recognises you. These entries are deleted automatically after 12 months. Please do not enter personal data in the chat.

To protect against misuse, we count how many questions per day come from one internet connection. For this we create a checksum from the IP address and a secret value that changes daily; the IP address cannot be worked out from it. These counters are deleted after 2 days at the latest.

For technical reasons our server receives your device’s IP address with every question. To prevent too many requests in a short time, it keeps it in memory for at most one minute; Supabase may also store it briefly (up to 7 days) in the server logs to keep the service running. We do not store it with your questions.

Only if you change the language (DE/EN) in the help or switch on “Read aloud” does it remember this choice in your browser’s storage, so that it stays as you chose next time. This is strictly necessary for a service you request (regulation 6(4) of the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003, PECR). There are no cookies and no tracking. You can remove the choice at any time in your browser settings (“clear website data”).

Our lawful basis for the processing described in this section is our legitimate interest in providing helpful, abuse-proof help for CarpD (Article 6(1)(f) UK GDPR); you can object under Article 21 UK GDPR – simply do not use the assistant. As we cannot link stored questions to a person, we cannot provide or delete them individually (Article 11 UK GDPR); they are deleted automatically after 12 months.

4d. Voice input and read-aloud in the assistant

If you press and hold the microphone button, the assistant uses the speech recognition of your browser or phone. Depending on the browser, your voice recording may be processed by the browser provider, e.g. Google (Chrome) or Apple (Safari) – partly on their servers. We only receive the recognised text, never the recording. The microphone is only active while you hold the button.

If you tap “Read aloud” on an answer, our own CarpD voice reads it first. For this our server (Supabase, Frankfurt) sends only the text of the assistant’s answer to the speech service Google Cloud Text-to-Speech (Google Cloud EMEA Limited, Dublin, Ireland), which turns it into audio – not your question, not your IP address and no other information about you. Google acts on our behalf (data processing agreement) and may also process the data in the USA; standard data protection clauses apply. The audio is only played, not stored. To keep costs under control we count, as in 4c, with a daily changing checksum (no IP address) how often read-aloud is used from one internet connection, and delete these counters after 2 days at the latest. If the limit is reached or the service is unavailable, your device’s own voice reads the answer.

For read-aloud with your device’s voice we prefer voices that run on the device itself. If there are none, the browser may use an online voice from its provider – the text read aloud then goes to that provider, and Google’s or Apple’s privacy terms apply.

6. The CarpD app

6.1 In short

CarpD stores everything you enter only on your phone. You do not need an account – there is no advertising and no tracking. Your photos, swims and notes are only transferred to our server if you use the cloud backup with an account – then privately, only for you (see 6.10). Only if you switch on “Anonymous statistics” are catches set to “Statistics” sent to our server – without swim, name or photos; only if you agree to “Usage statistics” are anonymous numbers on which features you use sent as well. You can create an account voluntarily (see 6.7).

6.2 What is stored on your phone

Catches (weight, species, bait, rig, note, time, weather and – if you allow it – the location), sessions, runs, swims, photos, licences with photos, planned trips, kit list and templates, and your settings.

In your profile you can optionally add a name, photo, year of birth and gender. These also stay on your phone; your name only appears on shared images if you switch this on.

If you delete CarpD, all this data is removed from your phone.

6.3 Permissions

Location: only while you use the app – to show you on the map, suggest the nearest water, calculate weather, sun and moon times for your location and save where a catch was made.

Camera and photos: for catch photos, photos of waters and licences, and your profile photo.

Notifications: weather alerts and reminders (e.g. before a trip or before a licence expires) are scheduled directly on your phone – without a server. Only crew messages come via our server (see 6.9).

All permissions are voluntary and can be changed at any time in your phone’s settings.

6.4 Services the app uses

For weather, map and waters, CarpD queries the following services. What is transmitted is the coordinates of the water, your location, the place of a catch or the map area – for planned trips the coordinates of the planned water – and, for technical reasons, your phone’s IP address. Your catches, photos and swims themselves are not transmitted.

  • Weather: Open-Meteo (Open-Meteo.com, Switzerland) – coordinates are rounded to about 1 km so that no exact location (e.g. your swim) is transmitted. The request goes via our server (Supabase, Frankfurt); Open-Meteo then receives only the rounded coordinates, not your IP address. If our server cannot be reached, the app asks Open-Meteo directly.
  • Waters: Overpass servers of FOSSGIS e. V. (Germany), OpenStreetMap France (France) and Private.coffee (Austria) – only for areas not included in the built-in waters package.
  • Place search: Geoapify (KEPTAGO LTD, Paphos, Cyprus; servers in the EU) – the request goes via our server (Supabase, Frankfurt); Geoapify receives only the search term, not your IP address or location. Only if that fails: Nominatim of the OpenStreetMap Foundation (United Kingdom).
  • Place names for unnamed waters (e.g. “lake near Waiblingen”): via your phone’s location service (Apple or Google), as a fallback via Nominatim.
  • Map: Apple Maps (iPhone) or Google Maps (Android).
  • Version check: when you open the app, it asks our server (Supabase, Frankfurt) whether this version is still recent enough. Only whether it is an iPhone or Android phone is transmitted (and, for technical reasons, the IP address); nothing is stored.

Our lawful basis is the provision of the app features you use (Article 6(1)(b) UK GDPR).

6.5 Backup and sharing

CarpD creates a backup as a file on your phone. Where you save it (e.g. iCloud Drive) or who you send it to is up to you, via your phone’s share menu. We do not receive the file. Please note: it also contains your swims. The automatic cloud backup with an account is described in 6.10.

Catch photos you share via CarpD contain no location.

6.6 Anonymous statistics (server at Supabase)

Only if you agree under More → Anonymous statistics does CarpD transmit catches set to “Statistics” (or “Public”) to our server. From these we create statistics for waters and a bite index that learns from the catches of many anglers.

Transmitted are: the water (OpenStreetMap ID and type of water – not the spot on the bank), date and hour, species, weight, bait, the weather at the time (pressure and trend, wind, air and estimated water temperature, cloud cover, precipitation), moon brightness, the bite index and the app version. Never transmitted: your name, your email address, the exact location (swim), photos, notes and the rig.

So that you can delete your data yourself, the app signs in to the server without a name or password and receives a random number for this. This number does not reveal who you are. For technical reasons the server receives your phone’s IP address with every connection; it is not stored with your catches. We only show statistics for a water once at least 5 different anglers have reported something there – and only in summary, never individual catches.

The server provider is Supabase, Inc. (USA); the data is stored in a data centre in Frankfurt am Main, Germany. We have a data processing agreement with Supabase; standard data protection clauses apply to any access from the USA.

Our lawful basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) UK GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time under More → Anonymous statistics – the app then deletes everything shared so far from the server (if you are offline, the next time you open it). If you set a catch to “Private” or delete it, it is also deleted from the server. “Delete my data from the server” removes everything at once. Otherwise we keep the data for as long as CarpD exists.

Usage statistics: Only if you agree at start-up or under More → Usage statistics does CarpD count on your phone which screens and features you open on a day (e.g. “session started: 2”, “feed calculator: 1”) and send these numbers once a day, together with “iPhone” or “Android” and the random number, to our server. There they are only added up across all devices; we keep the random number for at most 15 days so that a day is not counted twice. Catches, weights, baits, swims, location, name and account are never counted. Our lawful basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) UK GDPR; regulation 6 PECR). You can withdraw it at any time under More → Usage statistics; the app then also deletes all numbers not yet sent.

6.7 Account (optional)

Under More → Account you can create an account voluntarily – for features such as crews and cloud backup. Without an account, everything else in CarpD works as before.

You can sign in with Apple, with Google or with a code sent by email. We store: a random account number, your email address (with Apple, if you wish, a forwarding address created by Apple), the sign-in method, times of sign-up and last use, and the nickname you choose. We only store your real name if you expressly allow others to see it. There is no password.

When signing in with Apple or Google, we only receive your email address and – the first time – your name as a suggestion. Apple’s (Apple Distribution International Ltd., Ireland) or Google’s (Google Ireland Limited) privacy terms also apply. We send email codes via Mailjet (see above).

The account is stored on our server at Supabase, Inc. (USA) in the data centre in Frankfurt am Main; we have a data processing agreement with Supabase, and standard data protection clauses apply to any access from the USA. Other users only see what you share yourself – your nickname and, if you allow it, your profile picture. We do not pass on your data.

Our lawful basis is the provision of the features you use with your account (Article 6(1)(b) UK GDPR). “Delete account” (More → Account) deletes your account and everything linked to it from the server immediately. Everything on your phone stays. Anonymous sharing for the statistics is separate from the account and not linked to your name or email address.

6.8 Crews (closed groups)

With an account you can create crews or join them with an invitation code. A crew has at most 20 members. The other members see your nickname and – only if you allow it – your profile picture and real name. The profile picture is reduced in size and stored without location data in private storage that only your crews can read. The founder of a crew (boss) can make other members admins; the boss and admins can invite and remove members.

If you share a catch in a crew, we transmit: photo (reduced, without location and camera data), species, weight, the name of the water and the day. Never transmitted: your swim, coordinates, bait, rig and notes. You choose when the crew sees the catch (immediately, after the session or after 3, 7 or 14 days); until then nobody but you sees it. We also store who gave a catch a “Tight Lines”, and for each shared catch and trip a random identifier of your phone – so that entries are not overwritten if you use your account on two phones. It does not reveal which phone it is. Before sharing you can crop the photo and blur areas; your original stays unchanged on your phone.

If you show a planned trip in a crew, we only transmit the name of the water and the period – never swim, note, kit list or bait. We also store who tapped “I’m in”.

If you name an unnamed lake yourself and switch on “Visible to my crew” (off by default), we store the name, the lake’s OpenStreetMap ID, your account number and the time for that crew. Members then see the lake with this name and your nickname (“named by …”) – never your swim.

If you report a catch, we store who reported which catch for what reason and receive an email so that we can review the report within 24 hours. If you block someone, we store this so that you no longer see their catches and trips; the blocked person is not told.

What you share in a crew is seen only by the members of that crew – no other users and not the public. The data is stored on our server at Supabase in Frankfurt am Main (see 6.7).

Our lawful basis is the provision of the crew feature you use (Article 6(1)(b) UK GDPR). You can take a shared catch out of the crew or delete it at any time, leave the crew or – as boss – delete the crew; the shared content is then removed from the server. “Delete account” removes everything linked to your account.

6.9 Crew messages (push)

If you are in a crew with an account and allow notifications, you get a short message when someone in your crew shares a catch (e.g. “Kreso shared a catch: 12.4 kg”). For this your phone registers a device address (push token) with our server. We store this token, your account, whether it is an iPhone or Android phone, and the app language.

Our server sends the messages via Apple’s notification service (Apple Push Notification Service, Apple Inc., USA) or Google’s (Firebase Cloud Messaging, Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC, USA). Only the token and the text of the message are transmitted: the crew name, the nickname of the person sharing and the weight. Never transmitted: photos, waters, swims or locations. For any access from the USA, the UK-US data bridge and standard data protection clauses apply.

Our lawful basis is the provision of the crew feature you use (Article 6(1)(b) UK GDPR). You can switch off crew messages at any time under More → Settings → Notifications; the token is then deleted from the server immediately – likewise when you sign out and with “Delete account”. Tokens that Apple or Google report as invalid are deleted automatically.

6.10 Cloud backup

When you are signed in with an account, CarpD backs up your data automatically to our server: once a day when you open the app and after a session ends, on Wi-Fi only if you wish. You can also tap “Back up now” yourself.

The backup contains everything stored in CarpD – including photos, notes and the exact locations of your catches and swims. It is kept in private storage that only your account can access; other users and crews never see it. We do not look at it and do not pass it on.

We keep the last 3 backups; older ones are deleted automatically. A backup is only restored if you tap it yourself under More → Backup. The data is stored on our server at Supabase in Frankfurt am Main (see 6.7).

Our lawful basis is the provision of the cloud backup you use with your account (Article 6(1)(b) UK GDPR). “Delete account” also deletes all backups from the server immediately.

6.11 Founder number, founder price and CarpD Pro (subscription)

Test anglers and founders from the waiting list (see 4a and 4b) receive a founder number. If you create an account in the app, we link the number to your account via your email address or founder code and store the number and the time. This lets the app show your founder status (badge) and – for test anglers – the free year of CarpD Pro. We add individual people manually as “Pro for life”. Both are removed with “Delete account”. When you buy, renew, cancel or your subscription expires, RevenueCat tells our server (Supabase, Frankfurt am Main) which subscription belongs to your account, whether it is active and until when. We use this to show your founder badge and to avoid reminding you about the founder price if you already have it. This is also removed with “Delete account”.

You can buy CarpD Pro as a subscription via the App Store (Apple) or Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never receive your payment details (e.g. credit card). To manage subscriptions we use RevenueCat, Inc., San Francisco (USA). This involves: a random customer number or your account number, which subscription you have, purchase, renewal and expiry dates, price, currency and store country, and technical device details (model, system version, app version).

Our lawful basis is the performance of the subscription contract (Article 6(1)(b) UK GDPR). We have a data processing agreement with RevenueCat; RevenueCat is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework including the UK Extension, and standard data protection clauses also apply. Apple’s or Google’s privacy terms also apply to the purchase itself. We keep purchase data for as long as required by law (e.g. tax law).

6.12 Feedback by email

“Feedback & report a bug” opens your own email app with a message to test@carpd.de. It already contains the app version, system version and language so that we can trace errors. Only what you send yourself is sent.

We process your email address and message to handle your feedback (Article 6(1)(f) UK GDPR – legitimate interest in an error-free app). Our mailboxes are hosted by IONOS SE, Montabaur. We delete the messages at the latest 6 months after the end of the test.

6.12a Help & FAQ in the app

Under More → “Help & FAQ”, CarpD opens our help page carpd.de/hilfe in a browser window inside the app. The app itself transmits nothing except the page request. Sections 3, 4c and 4d apply to the page and the AI assistant.

6.13 Crash and error reports (Sentry)

So that we can find and fix errors, CarpD automatically reports crashes and errors to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc., USA). We have chosen data storage in the EU (Frankfurt).

Only technical information is transmitted: the error message and the place in the code, app version, device model, system version, language and time. Not transmitted: locations (coordinates are removed beforehand), catches, swims, photos, names, the IP address or screenshots.

Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in a stable, error-free app (Article 6(1)(f) UK GDPR). We have a data processing agreement with Sentry; for transfers to the USA, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework including the UK Extension and standard data protection clauses apply. Reports are deleted after 90 days at the latest.

6.14 Test version via TestFlight and Google Play

You receive the test version via TestFlight (Apple Distribution International Ltd., Ireland) or Google Play (Google Ireland Limited). These services may pass on crash reports, technical device information and feedback you send there to us. Apple’s or Google’s privacy terms also apply.

7. International transfers

We are based in Germany, and our server is in Frankfurt am Main. Transfers of your data from the UK to the European Economic Area are covered by the UK adequacy regulations for the EEA. Where service providers in the USA may access data (see above), we rely on the UK-US data bridge (UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework) where the provider is certified, and otherwise on the International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU standard contractual clauses (IDTA/UK Addendum). Switzerland, where Open-Meteo is based, is also recognised by the UK as providing adequate protection.

8. Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you have the right to access your personal data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict processing, to data portability and to object to processing (Articles 15–21 UK GDPR). Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. Just write to us at info@carpd.de. We will respond within one month.

You also have the right to complain to the UK supervisory authority, the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO): Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire SK9 5AF, ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint. We would, however, appreciate the chance to deal with your concerns first, so please contact us. You can delete anonymously shared data yourself at any time: More → Anonymous statistics → Delete my data from the server.

We do not make decisions based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects on you, and we do not sell your data.

9. Deleting your CarpD account and data

In the app: More → Account → “Delete account” and confirm. Your account is deleted immediately – together with your profile (nickname, real name, profile picture), the founder number on your account, crews you created, catches, photos and trips shared in crews, “Tight Lines”, reports, blocks, device addresses for crew messages and all cloud backups. What is stored on your phone stays there.

Without the app: write to us from your account’s email address at info@carpd.de with the subject “Delete CarpD account”. We delete the account with all the data listed above within 30 days and confirm this by email.

Deleting individual data only: you can take shared catches and trips out of a crew individually at any time. You delete anonymously shared statistics data under More → Anonymous statistics → “Delete my data from the server”. Crash reports (6.13) are deleted after 90 days at the latest.

The rules for crews and the other conditions are set out in the terms of use.
